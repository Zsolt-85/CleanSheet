# Outbound sprint — copy + search strings

Working files for the stranger-reach motion. Not a post package: nothing here
gets published to your feed. The point is to get your name in front of people
whose job involves messy lists.

Context (9/29 export): +7 followers this week came from being seen in comments
and profiles, not from Post 1 (411 imp) or Post 2 (8 imp). This motion is the
growth engine, not a consolation prize.

---

## 1. Connection notes

Under LinkedIn's 300-char limit. The tool is mentioned once, in the last
sentence. The first sentence is about *them*.

**A — recruiters / talent acquisition** (226 chars)

```
Hi {Name} - I build a small free tool that cleans messy contact lists
(duplicates, broken rows) before they hit your ATS. Nothing stored, no
signup. If a list ever slows you down, send it over and I will clean it
free. - Zsolt
```

**B — VAs / agency ops** (227 chars)

```
Hi {Name} - I made a free tool that cleans duplicate and messy rows out of
the contact lists VAs and agencies juggle. No signup, files are not stored.
Sending one your way to try? Happy to tell you exactly what changed. - Zsolt
```

**C — sales ops / RevOps** (245 chars)

```
Hi {Name} - one thing I hear often in sales ops: the list is fine until the
CRM is not. I built a small free cleaner for exactly that (duplicates,
broken rows, nothing stored). Not selling anything, just curious how your
team handles it. - Zsolt
```

Rules:
- Blank invites convert worst. Always send a note.
- Send **A or B or C** by role. One note fits one person; re-using the same
  text across 15 people is how accounts get flagged.
- No link in the note. Your profile is the landing surface.
- Never mention the posts. They have not worked; do not point at them.

## 2. Finding people

LinkedIn people search, one query per group. Run them, open results, and send
**5 notes today** — 10 by Friday.

**Recruiters / TA**
```
Recruiter AND ("contact list" OR "candidate database" OR "sourcing")
"talent acquisition" AND (CRM OR "Bullhorn" OR "Greenhouse" OR "Workable")
Recruiter AND (Romania OR Hungary OR "remote" OR EMEA)
```

**VAs / agency ops**
```
"virtual assistant" AND ("lead generation" OR "data entry" OR "list building")
"operations coordinator" AND (agency OR "marketing agency")
"operations manager" AND "staffing"
```

**Sales ops / RevOps**
```
"sales operations" AND (CRM OR Salesforce OR HubSpot)
revops AND ("data quality" OR "duplicate" OR "territory")
"sales enablement" AND "lead list"
```

Where the people actually are (LinkedIn is thin on these; use them too):
- Recruiter communities and VA/agency groups — comment, don't pitch.
- `#revops`, `#salesops`, `#recruiting`, `#virtualassistant` on LinkedIn.
- X and Reddit for the same titles; these are stranger-reachable and LinkedIn
  is currently throttling you.

## 3. Daily warming comments — 3 a day

This is what produced the +7. Skip it and the connects go cold.

Find posts in the sales-ops / VA / recruiting corners with a few comments from
people you don't know. Comment early. Structure:

```
[specific thing they said] + [your relevant experience or number] + [a real question]
```

Worked shape — *not* filler to copy:

> That double-import problem is worse when the ATS reformats the email column
> on export. We hit 1 in 50 rows coming back with a suffix, so every dedupe
> rule has a loophole. How are you catching yours before it reaches the CRM?

Hard rules:
- 2–4 sentences, one idea, ends in a question.
- No link. No "great post". No mention of your tool.
- Name something specific from *their* post or you have not read it.
- Reply inside the thread, not to the top-level post.

## 4. After they accept

Accept alone is not a signal. Do not send the tool.

Send one question instead:

```
Thanks for connecting. Out of curiosity - what is the ugliest list you have
had to clean up recently? I have been collecting patterns and they are
wilder than you would think.
```

Why: profile-visits-then-post worked; a pitch in the inbox does not. Their
answer is the discovery interview you have been missing, and it costs one
message.

Then: if they describe a mess, send the file. If they go quiet, do nothing.
