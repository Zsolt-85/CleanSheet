# STATUS — closed as a business

**Decision, 2026-11-22:** CleanSheet is no longer being developed or promoted
as a business. The app stays live at `https://cleansheet-s4vk.onrender.com/`
as a working portfolio artifact. No further features, posts, or outreach.

## Why

- Two LinkedIn posts, ~470 impressions, **0 link clicks, 0 files run by a stranger**.
- Only two DMs ever. The core hypothesis — that strangers with genuinely
  messy lists would use this — was never actually tested.
- The failure was distribution: the posts reached a personal network with no
  list-cleaning pain. That is a channel/audience problem, not a code problem.
- Market judgment: routine list cleanup is increasingly absorbed by built-in
  spreadsheet AI/Copilot features inside larger organizations. The remaining
  privacy-sensitive niche was real but too narrow to reach with the channel
  available.
- The spending ceiling was never justified by evidence, so it was not spent.

Keep the framing accurate: the tool was **not disproved — it was never
distributed to buyers**. "Built and shipped a working tool; couldn't reach a
market for it" is the honest version and the one worth telling.

## What stays

- Live app, source, tests (102 passing), content history, and all notes.
- The existing feature freeze becomes permanent.

## Reopen only if

A specific person with recurring list pain asks for it, or a route to that
audience appears that does not require paid acquisition or cold outbound.
Otherwise this document is the final word — no re-analysis needed.
